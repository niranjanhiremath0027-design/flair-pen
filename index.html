The Flair story

From 1,000 metal pens a day to one of India's largest writing instrument makers.

1976

Metal pens in Mumbai

Khubilal Jugraj Rathod began manufacturing metal pens, selling them for ₹1–1.50 across Mumbai, Delhi's Sadar Bazar, Chennai, and Bengaluru.

1990

The shift to plastic

Flair expanded into plastic ballpoint pens as the market moved beyond metal barrels.

1998

Daman manufacturing plant

A dedicated production facility opened in Daman to scale up output.

2009

Dehradun expansion

A second major plant opened in Uttarakhand, growing national manufacturing capacity.

2018

Flair Writing Industries Ltd

The company converted to a public limited company, now selling under Flair, Hauser, Pierre Cardin, and ZOOX.

2023

Public listing

Flair Writing Industries went public, having sold 1.3 billion+ pens that year, a quarter of them exported worldwide.
